How to use this tool (instructions, required columns, outputs)

Workflow at a glance

  1. Step 1: prepare one Excel workbook with sheets named exactly Books and 2B, plus an optional 3B sheet, and upload it. A blank template can be downloaded from the top of the page, just above Step 1.
  2. Step 2: click Run Reconciliation. Summaries appear below in three tabs: Books ↔ 2B, 2B ↔ 3B and Books ↔ 3B.
  3. Download the reports using the buttons under Step 2 or under each summary.

Everything runs in your browser and no data is uploaded. The page needs an internet connection only to load its Excel libraries. This version is intended for one GSTIN (registration) at a time, so use a separate workbook for each GSTIN.

Preparing the workbook

Keep the column headings exactly as in the template. Books and 2B need these columns:

  • GSTIN of supplier, Trade/Legal Name, Invoice Number, Invoice Date
  • Taxable Value (₹), Integrated Tax(₹), Central Tax(₹), State/UT Tax(₹), Cess(₹)
  • Month – the accounting month in Books, the ITC availability period in 2B and the claim month in 3B. Enter it as DD/MM/YYYY; it is kept as text in the output.

The optional 3B sheet uses the same columns plus 3B Table 4 Reference and Nature of ITC. Choose Nature of ITC from the dropdown in the template:

  • ITC on Import of Goods [3B-T4-A1] · ITC on Import for Services [3B-T4-A2] · ITC on RCM [3B-T4-A3] · ITC Through ISD [3B-T4-A4] · All Other ITC [3B-T4-A5]
  • Permanent Reversal [3B-T4-B1] · Temporary Reversal [3B-T4-B2]
  • (Reclaim of Reversed Temporary All Other ITC [3B-T4-D1]) · Reclaim All Other ITC [3B-T4-A5]

The Reversal and Reclaim ITC sheet includes only invoices with a B2 reversal or D1 reclaim, summarized using tax heads for their A5, B2 and D1 entries. It calculates A5 - D1 - B2 for each tax head; check invoice claim details wherever the difference is not zero. D1 and B2 are treated as positive amounts to subtract. The separate Net ITC claimed in 3B sheet gives overall totals by Nature of ITC, with net ITC equal to A1-A5 less B1 and B2; D1 is excluded there.

Leave the 3B sheet blank if the ITC claimed in 3B is NIL.

Tips. Dates can be real Excel dates or text such as 05/04/2025. Amounts should be numbers with no more than 2 decimal places (commas and ₹ signs are tolerated). Use one row per invoice line; rows with the same GSTIN, invoice number and invoice date are combined automatically before matching.

How matching works

Entries are matched in this order. Once matched, an entry is not used again.

PassWhat must agree
Fully MatchedSame supplier GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date and all five amounts
Partial Match 1 – Invoice NumberSame GSTIN, invoice date and all amounts; the invoice number differs
Partial Match 2 – Invoice DateSame GSTIN, invoice number and all amounts; the invoice date differs
Partial Match 3 – AmountSame GSTIN, invoice number and date; amounts differ by more than ₹1
Difference ≤ 1 RupeeSame invoice number and date, same GSTIN (or same PAN); amounts differ by ₹1 or less
GSTIN/PANSame invoice number, date and amounts, with a different GSTIN but the same PAN (for example, another state registration of the same supplier)

Invoice numbers are compared ignoring capitals, spaces and punctuation (INV-001 = inv 001).

Reading the results

  • Books ↔ 2B – the main reconciliation. The report has 11 sheets: Summary, Amount Difference, the consolidated sheet, one sheet per match pass, 2B Mismatched, Book Mismatched and Name Comparison.
  • 2B ↔ 3B and Books ↔ 3B – the same checks between ITC available (2B) or booked (Books) and ITC claimed in 3B.
  • The Consolidated Sheet lists all Books, 2B and 3B entries with their reconciliation status. The separate Other Details of ITC file contains Early Claim of ITC, invoice-level A5/B2/D1 tax details and A5 - D1 - B2 differences for invoices with a B2 reversal or D1 reclaim, and overall Net ITC totals by Nature of ITC (A1-A5 less B1/B2; D1 excluded).
  • The Name Comparison file compares supplier names in Books and 2B by GSTIN, to spot name differences.
  • In the summary tables, Entries are combined invoices and Rows are the original Excel rows. Unmatched rows are the ones to investigate first.

Suggested review order

  1. Check that Input totals equal your source sheets.
  2. Review 2B Mismatched (ITC available but not booked) and Book Mismatched (booked but not in 2B).
  3. Review the partial-match sheets for wrong invoice numbers, dates or amounts.
  4. Review the 3B tabs against what was claimed.

Common problems

  • “Sheet … is missing columns” – a heading was renamed or removed. Use the template headings exactly.
  • “Sheet … is empty” – the Books or 2B sheet has no rows, or the sheet name is wrong.
  • Everything shows as unmatched – check the GSTIN column and that Books and 2B belong to the same registration and period.
  • Buttons do nothing / libraries not loaded – check the internet connection and reload the page.
