How to use this tool (instructions, Section Codes, B/f & C/f)

Workflow at a glance

  1. Step 1: upload the ATS .txt file(s), convert them, then use the download buttons for the converted Excel workbook and the TAN & Section-wise TDS / TCS summary.
  2. Step 2: download the Books Data template, fill it with your Books entries, and upload it.
  3. Step 3: run the reconciliation, then use the download button to save the reconciliation workbook. A summary also appears below Step 3.

Everything runs in your browser. Use a current Chrome or Edge. Dates in all outputs are dd/mm/yyyy and all columns are auto-fitted.

Step 1 – what the converted workbook contains

  • Master Data – name, PAN, tax year etc. of each statement.
  • Part Summary and Section Wise Summary – entries, amount and TDS/TCS (Parts VII–X are hidden in the summaries).
  • PART-I, PART-IV … – detailed entries, one sheet per Part. Deductor details are repeated on each child row.
  • PART-X – both sub-tables (Tax Year summary and TAN/PAN breakdown) are combined into one table with each column shown once.
  • Reconciliation – equal positive/negative entries that cancelled each other, and negative entries that could not be paired.

Step 2 – filling the Books template

Sheet “Books Data” (keep the headings exactly): Type of Tax (TDS / TCS), Name of Deductor, TAN of Deductor, PAN of Deductor, Section, Invoice Date, Invoice Number, Amount Paid / Credited, Tax Deducted / Deposited, Branch Name, B/f or C/f.

  • Give TAN, or PAN if TAN is not available. Amounts must be numbers.
  • Dates: dd/mm/yyyy, dd-Mon-yyyy or real Excel dates.
  • The Invoice Number column must exist but is not used for matching and is not shown in the output.
  • Type B/f in “B/f or C/f” for entries brought forward from last year. Leave blank for all others.

Sheet “26AS B-f Data” (only if you have B/f entries): Type of Tax, Name of Deductor, TAN, PAN, Section, Transaction Date, Amount Paid / Credited, Tax Deducted / Deposited.

B/f and C/f. B/f entries are in Books but not in this year's 26AS text file, so enter their 26AS side in “26AS B-f Data”. B/f Books entries are matched only with that sheet; all other Books entries are matched only with the Step 1 text file. C/f entries are 26AS entries you will book next year: after reconciliation they appear in “Not Matched in Books”, and you mark them C/f yourself.

Section Codes

The 2026-27 statement shows a numeric Section Code, which this tool converts into the section wording (list built into this file from Section.xlsx; nothing needs to be uploaded).

Section Code from Tax Year 2026-27Section
1002392-Employee Other Than Government Employee
1010393(1) [Table Sl. No. 3(i)]
1029393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 7]
  • Write the Section in Books exactly as in column A of Section.xlsx. Capitals, spaces and punctuation are ignored (Sl. No. 1 (i) = Sl.No.1(i)), but different words are not.
  • If only the Section differs, the entry goes to “Matched - Section Mismatch” instead of “Full Match”.
  • To change the list: keep a backup, open this HTML in a text editor (not Word), search for TAX_YEAR_SECTION_CODES, edit entries like ["1029","393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 7]"], save, and test with one statement.

Step 3 – matching rules

An entry matches when TAN (or PAN if TAN is missing), Amount and TDS/TCS amount agree. If the Section also agrees it is a Full Match; if not, Matched - Section Mismatch. There is no partial match. Parts VII–X are not reconciled. Each entry is used once.

Common problems

  • “Missing required columns” – a heading in the Books file was renamed or removed; use the template headings.
  • Reconcile button disabled – complete Step 1 and Step 2 in the same session; repeat both if you change a file.
  • Many Section Mismatches – Books section wording differs from Section.xlsx; standardise it with find-and-replace.
  • Entry should match but does not – check TAN/PAN, and the amount and TDS to the paisa.

Section-wise summary

Totals use retained entries and exclude Parts VII, VIII, IX, and X. Columns remain visible even when a value is blank.

Every negative entry is removed together with its closest positive entry in the same table, so both are excluded from that part’s detail sheet. An exact equal-amount positive is used first (preferring the same section/date, then the nearest row); if none exists, the positive with the nearest amount is used and the difference is recorded. Every matched pair and any unmatched negative entries are listed in the workbook’s Reconciliation sheet.
