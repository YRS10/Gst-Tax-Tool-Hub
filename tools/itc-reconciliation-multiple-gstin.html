How to use this tool (instructions, multiple GSTIN, required columns, outputs)

Workflow at a glance

  1. Step 1: prepare one Excel workbook with sheets named exactly Books and 2B, plus an optional 3B sheet, and upload it. A blank template can be downloaded from the top of the page, just above Step 1.
  2. Step 2: click Run Reconciliation. Summaries appear below in three tabs: Books ↔ 2B, 2B ↔ 3B and Books ↔ 3B.
  3. Download the reports using the buttons under Step 2 or under each summary.

Everything runs in your browser and no data is uploaded. The page needs an internet connection only to load its Excel libraries. This version handles several recipient GSTINs in one workbook.

Preparing the workbook

Keep the column headings exactly as in the template. Books and 2B need these columns:

  • GSTIN of Recipient – the first column in all three sheets. This is your own GSTIN that the invoice belongs to (the registration receiving the ITC). Blank or missing recipient GSTINs default to 07AAAAA1234D1L1.
  • GSTIN of supplier, Trade/Legal Name, Invoice Number, Invoice Date
  • Taxable Value (₹), Integrated Tax(₹), Central Tax(₹), State/UT Tax(₹), Cess(₹)
  • Month – the accounting month in Books, the ITC availability period in 2B and the claim month in 3B. Enter it as DD/MM/YYYY; it is kept as text in the output.

The optional 3B sheet uses the same columns plus 3B Table 4 Reference and Nature of ITC. Choose Nature of ITC from the dropdown in the template:

  • ITC on Import of Goods [3B-T4-A1] · ITC on Import for Services [3B-T4-A2] · ITC on RCM [3B-T4-A3] · ITC Through ISD [3B-T4-A4] · All Other ITC [3B-T4-A5]
  • Permanent Reversal [3B-T4-B1] · Temporary Reversal [3B-T4-B2]
  • (Reclaim of Reversed Temporary All Other ITC [3B-T4-D1]) · Reclaim All Other ITC [3B-T4-A5]

The Reversal and Reclaim ITC sheet includes only invoices with a B2 reversal or D1 reclaim (B1 permanent reversals are not shown), summarized using tax heads for their A5, A1-A4, B2 and D1 entries. As per law B2 and D1 relate only to A5, so it calculates A5 - D1 - B2 for each tax head; A1-A4 amounts are shown for information and are never matched with B2/D1, so a B2 or D1 wrongly entered against an A1-A4 invoice appears as a difference; a Reclaim Status column flags invoices reclaimed via D1 whose B2 reversal was made in an earlier period (not in this data); check invoice claim details wherever the difference is not zero. D1 and B2 are treated as positive amounts to subtract. Tax heads are clubbed under grouped headings (ITC Claimed, ITC Reversal, ITC Reclaimed, Difference), each split into Integrated / Central / State-UT / Cess. The Permanent Reversal (B1) ITC sheet lists only invoices with a B1 entry, showing their A1-A5 claim against the B1 reversal, and ties the B1 total to the B1 row of the Net ITC sheet; the B2 total of the Reversal and Reclaim ITC sheet is tied to the B2 row in the same way. The separate Net ITC claimed in 3B sheet gives the grand total by Nature of ITC, with net ITC equal to A1-A5 less B1 and B2 (D1 is excluded there), followed below by the same table for each month of the 3B sheet.

Leave the 3B sheet blank if the ITC claimed in 3B is NIL.

Suppliers without a GSTIN (imports and unregistered RCM)

Leave GSTIN of supplier blank only in the three cases below, and choose the Supplier Type from the dropdown (the last column of every sheet). The tool then assigns an internal code so these rows are not merged with each other. A blank GSTIN in any other row stops the run with a message naming the sheet and row.

  • On the 3B sheet, if Supplier Type is left blank, Nature of ITC decides the code (A1 = IMPG, A2 = IMPS, A3 = URP). RCM from a registered supplier uses the real GSTIN.
  • These rows are identified only by invoice number and date, so every document needs a unique number; use the same number and date in Books and 3B.
  • Import of Services and unregistered RCM are not reported in 2B, so they are left out of the Books ↔ 2B and 2B ↔ 3B tabs and compared in Books ↔ 3B only. A note at the top of the results shows how many entries were set aside.
  • These rows are not PAN-matched and do not appear in the Name Comparison file.
  • RCM credit is available only after the tax is paid in cash, so the 3B month can be later than the Books month.

Tips. Dates can be real Excel dates or text such as 05/04/2025. Amounts should be numbers with no more than 2 decimal places (commas and ₹ signs are tolerated). Use one row per invoice line; rows with the same recipient GSTIN, supplier GSTIN, invoice number and invoice date are combined automatically before matching. Use the same spelling of the recipient GSTIN in Books, 2B and 3B; capitals and spaces are ignored.

How matching works

Entries are compared only within the same GSTIN of Recipient. Seven passes run in this order, and once matched an entry is not used again.

PassWhat must agree
Fully MatchedSame recipient and supplier GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date and all five amounts
Partial Match 1 – Invoice NumberSame GSTIN, invoice date and all amounts; the invoice number differs
Partial Match 2 – Invoice DateSame GSTIN, invoice number and all amounts; the invoice date differs
Partial Match 3 – AmountSame GSTIN, invoice number and date; amounts differ by more than ₹1
Difference ≤ 1 RupeeSame invoice number and date, same supplier GSTIN (or same PAN); amounts differ by ₹1 or less
GSTIN/PANSame invoice number, date and amounts, with a different supplier GSTIN but the same PAN
Recipient GSTIN DifferentThe only pass that crosses recipients: same supplier GSTIN, invoice number, date and amounts, but booked under a different GSTIN of Recipient. Use it to find invoices posted or claimed under the wrong registration

Invoice numbers are compared ignoring capitals, spaces and punctuation (INV-001 = inv 001).

Reading the results on screen

  • Each tab shows a GSTIN of Recipient dropdown above the summary. The default is All Recipients (combined); choose one GSTIN to see only its figures. The dropdown is only needed when there are several GSTINs. Downloaded reports always include every GSTIN, whatever is selected.
  • When the workbook has more than one recipient GSTIN, a recipient snapshot table lists All Recipients and each GSTIN with its entry counts, matched pairs, unmatched counts and taxable and tax differences. The Status column shows Reconciled or Review, so a GSTIN that does not agree stands out. Click a row to select that GSTIN in the summary.
  • In the summary tables, Entries are combined invoices and Rows are the original Excel rows. Unmatched rows are the ones to investigate first.

Reading the downloaded reports

  • Books ↔ 2B report (14 sheets): Summary, Amount Difference, Recipient Snapshot, the consolidated sheet, one sheet per match pass, 2B Mismatched, Book Mismatched and Name Comparison. Every sheet starts with the GSTIN of Recipient column, and the Summary has a block for All Recipients followed by one block per GSTIN.
  • 2B ↔ 3B and Books ↔ 3B reports: the same checks between ITC available (2B) or booked (Books) and ITC claimed in 3B.
  • Consolidated Sheet (Step 2 download): all Books, 2B and 3B entries with their status and the Recipient GSTIN Difference sheet. Recipient GSTIN Difference shows the Books vs 2B vs 3B difference by amount head and invoices under different recipient GSTINs in different sources.
  • Other Details of ITC file: separate sheets for Early Claim of ITC, invoice-level A5/B2/D1 tax details and A5 - D1 - B2 differences for invoices with B2 or D1 activity, and overall Net ITC claimed in 3B by Nature of ITC (A1-A5 less B1/B2; D1 excluded).
  • Name Comparison file: compares supplier names in Books and 2B by GSTIN, to spot name differences.

Suggested review order

  1. Check the recipient snapshot first: any GSTIN marked Review needs a look before the detail.
  2. Review Recipient GSTIN Different matches and the Recipient GSTIN Difference sheet for invoices under the wrong registration.
  3. Review 2B Mismatched (ITC available but not booked) and Book Mismatched (booked but not in 2B).
  4. Review the partial-match sheets for wrong invoice numbers, dates or amounts, then the 3B tabs against what was claimed.

Common problems

  • “Sheet … is missing columns” – a required heading was renamed or removed. Use the template headings exactly; if the recipient GSTIN column is missing or blank, the default 07AAAAA1234D1L1 is applied.
  • “Sheet … is empty” – the Books or 2B sheet has no rows, or the sheet name is wrong.
  • Many invoices fall in “Recipient GSTIN Different”, or everything is unmatched – recipient GSTINs differ between sheets. Enter them consistently on every row, or leave all recipient values blank to use the default.
  • Buttons do nothing / libraries not loaded – check the internet connection and reload the page.
